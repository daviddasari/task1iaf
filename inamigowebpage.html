Who We Are

A Certified Non-Profit Driving Nationwide Change

Founded by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, licensed by the Central Government. We operate transparently with 80G & 12A certifications, ensuring tax-exempt benefits for every donor.

Our work spans food distribution, education, women's empowerment, animal care, environmental conservation, and skill development — all aligned with national development goals through NITI Aayog registration.

✔ Section 8 Registered ✔ 80G & 12A Certified ✔ CSR-1 Registered ✔ NITI Aayog ✔ IAF ISO 9001:2015

Bridging educational gaps through digital literacy, life skills training, and school education support for underprivileged children.

Quality education for thousands of children
